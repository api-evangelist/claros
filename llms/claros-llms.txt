# Claros

> Claros is a data center power delivery company reimagining how energy moves from chip to grid for AI and high-performance computing infrastructure. It builds an Integrated Voltage Regulator (IVR) for chip-level power delivery and a Power Gateway for data center power distribution. Early-stage hardware company (Torrance, CA / McLean, VA) backed by General Catalyst and Red Cell Partners. As of 2026 it is pre-product and publishes no developer API, SDK, or documentation surface.
